# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Header Scout.

1. Open an issue describing the change or bug before a larger change.
2. Keep the tool dependency-free and limited to one explicitly supplied URL.
3. Do not add crawling, credential collection, or automatic redirect following.
4. Never include real secrets, cookies, or private target details in issues or examples.
5. Describe how you checked your change and include sanitized sample output where useful.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions are provided under the repository's MIT license.
